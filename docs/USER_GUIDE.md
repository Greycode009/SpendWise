# SpendWise user guide

SpendWise answers five questions: **Where did my money go? How much do I have?
Who owes me? Whom do I owe? Am I saving enough?**

## Getting started

1. Open SpendWise and **Create an account** (name, email, password, currency).
2. On Android, tap **Install** when Chrome offers it (or ⋮ → *Install app*).
3. You'll land on **Home**. Everything you add from now on is saved on your
   phone first, so it works with no internet.

## Adding money in and out — the **+** button

The big **+** in the middle of the bottom bar opens *Add entry*. Choose:

| Tab | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Expense** | anything you spent: amount → category → (description) → Save |
| **Income** | salary, business, freelance, gifts… |
| **Lent** | you gave someone money they'll return |
| **Borrowed** | someone gave you money you'll return |

Tips for speed:
- The amount field is focused automatically with a number keypad.
- **Today / Yesterday** chips set the date in one tap.
- **Save & add another** keeps the screen open for several entries.
- Long-press the app icon → *Add expense* to jump straight in.

## Home

- **Balance** — cash you have: income − expenses, adjusted for money lent/borrowed and repaid.
- **Net worth** — balance plus money owed to you minus money you owe.
- **Income / Spent** this month, **Owed to you**, **You owe**.
- Spending donut for the month, recent activity, and loans **due soon**.

## Activity

All income and expenses grouped by day, with day totals. Switch months with
‹ ›, filter Expenses/Income, or search by description, note or category. Tap
an entry to edit or delete it.

## People — lending & borrowing

- The list shows everyone with an open balance ("owes you" / "you owe").
- Tap a person to see their loans, add a new one (**Lend** / **Borrow**), edit
  their details or delete them.
- Tap a loan → **Record money received** (or *Record repayment*) to log a
  partial or full repayment. **Full** fills in the whole outstanding amount.
- Loan status: *Open* · *Partly repaid* · *Overdue* (past its due date) · *Settled*.

## Insights

Per month: income, spending (with change vs last month), amount saved and
**savings rate**, spending by category, 6-month income vs expenses, and daily
spending.

## Settings

- Name and **currency** (NPR, INR, USD, EUR, GBP and more).
- **Theme**: Auto / Light / Dark.
- **Categories**: add your own (icon + colour), rename or delete.
- **Sync status**: what is waiting to sync, retry, or discard a failed change.
- **Install** and **Log out**.

## Offline & sync — what the icons mean

| You see | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Synced** badge | Everything is on the server |
| **N pending** | N changes saved on the phone, about to be sent |
| **Offline · N to sync** | No internet; changes are safe on the phone |
| ☁︎↑ next to an item | That item hasn't reached the server yet |
| **N failed** | The server refused a change (e.g. the loan was deleted on another device). Open *Sync status* to retry or discard it |
| **Sign in to sync** | Your login expired; sign in again — nothing is lost |

Using SpendWise on two devices (phone + laptop) with the same account keeps both
in step automatically. If the same entry is edited on both while offline, the
most recent edit wins; a deleted item stays deleted.

## Privacy

Your records are visible only to your account. Logging out removes your data
from that device (it stays safe on the server). If you have unsynced changes,
SpendWise warns you first.
